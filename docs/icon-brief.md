# Button icon brief

The brief the icons in `driver/icons` were made from, given to an image generator in November 2026. The generator drew a
sheet of all 29; 22 were cut out and ship with JarnGrip (the seven chat channels keep their names on the pad).

```text
Design a set of 29 original button icons for a touchscreen control pad used to play World of Warcraft. The pad runs on the second screen of a handheld gaming PC. Each icon sits on a large rectangular button, to the left of or above a short text label (for example, the "Map" button shows a map icon and the word "Map"). The app recolors the icons to match its themes: stone and gold, slate and blue, pure black, and frosted glass over a picture. So the icons must be pure shape, with no color of their own.

TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS (strict)
- One PNG file per icon, 128 x 128 pixels. If you can produce SVG instead, use a 24 x 24 viewBox and give both.
- Pure white (#FFFFFF) shapes on a fully transparent background. No other colors, gray tones, gradients, shadows, glows, outlines in other colors, or background shapes.
- Keep a 12 px empty margin on every side: the artwork fits inside a 104 x 104 px area, centered.
- Line style: strokes about 10 px thick at 128 px (2 px on a 24 px grid), rounded ends and corners. Small solid fills are fine (a dot, a coin, a solid head), as long as they keep the same visual weight as the lines.
- No text, letters or numbers in any icon, except where noted below.
- Each icon must still read clearly when shrunk to 48 x 48 px. Prefer bold, simple silhouettes over detail.
- All 29 must look like one set: the same line thickness, corner rounding, overall size and level of detail.

STYLE
- Clean, modern line icons with a light fantasy flavor: think a medieval adventure game drawn in the style of a minimal icon set. Not realistic, not cartoonish, not pixel art.
- Every design must be your own. Do not copy, trace or closely imitate Blizzard's World of Warcraft icons, logos, faction symbols or interface art, or any other game's artwork.

THE ICONS (file name: what the button does, then a suggested idea that you may improve)

Interface page
1. character.png: character sheet and equipment. A helmet, or a figure in armor.
2. spellbook.png: the spellbook. A closed book with a spark or star on the cover.
3. talents.png: talent and skill choices. A small branching tree of three or four connected nodes.
4. quest_log.png: the quest journal. A scroll with an exclamation mark (the one exception to "no text").
5. map.png: the world map. A folded map with a route marker.
6. social.png: friends list. Two overlapping people.
7. guild.png: the player's guild. A banner or shield on a pole.
8. group_finder.png: finding a group for a dungeon. A magnifying glass over a small group of people.
9. achievements.png: achievements. A shield with a star, or a medal.
10. professions.png: crafting professions. Crossed hammer and pick, or an anvil.
11. bags.png: inventory. A drawstring pouch or backpack.
12. system.png: the game menu (settings, log out). A cog wheel.

Target page
13. self.png: target yourself. A single person inside a target ring or crosshair.
14. party.png: target a party member. A single person with a smaller second person behind. The same icon is used for party members 1 to 4, and the button's label carries the number.
15. next_enemy.png: target the next enemy. A crosshair with an arrow pointing right.
16. previous_enemy.png: target the previous enemy. The same crosshair with an arrow pointing left (a mirror of next_enemy).

Utility page
17. sheathe_weapons.png: draw or put away weapons. A sword going into a scabbard.
18. screenshot.png: take a screenshot. A simple camera.
19. sit_stand.png: sit down or stand up. A seated figure, or a figure next to a stool.
20. chat.png: open the chat menu. A large speech bubble. This one appears bigger than the rest, on a round button about 300 px across, so it may have slightly more presence, but the same line style.
21. chat_page_up.png: scroll the chat window up. A speech bubble with an up arrow.
22. chat_page_down.png: scroll the chat window down. The same bubble with a down arrow.

Chat channels (shown around the chat button like the petals of a flower; all seven share one speech-bubble shape and differ only by a small mark inside or beside it)
23. chat_say.png: talk to people nearby. The plain bubble.
24. chat_yell.png: shout to everyone around. The bubble with sound lines or a jagged edge.
25. chat_whisper.png: a private message. A small bubble with a dotted outline, or a bubble with a lock.
26. chat_party.png: talk to your group. The bubble with two small people.
27. chat_raid.png: talk to your raid. The bubble with three small people, or a small banner.
28. chat_guild.png: talk to your guild. The bubble with the banner or shield from guild.png.
29. chat_instance.png: talk to your dungeon group. The bubble with a small doorway or archway.

DELIVERABLES
- The 29 PNG files, named exactly as above.
- One preview sheet showing all 29 icons in a grid, in white on a dark gray background (#2A2A2A), each labeled with its file name below it. This sheet is only for checking the set at a glance, so it can have a background.


```
